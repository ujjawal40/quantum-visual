The photoelectric effect

Shine light on a metal and electrons can fly off it. But only the right colour works: the dimmest violet light frees electrons that the brightest red light never can. Explaining why won Einstein his Nobel prize.

Light from the lamp hits the metal plate on the left. Any electrons it frees fly across the vacuum to the collector on the right, and the meter counts them.

The lamp is on. Change the colour, brightness or metal and watch what the plate does.
